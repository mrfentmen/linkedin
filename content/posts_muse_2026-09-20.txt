I have several phones running Meta's Muse and none of them needed a laptop to set up. The muse-relay that links them together was written by Muse itself. I gave it the problem and it built the plumbing that lets one chat board drive every phone at once.

Muse told me it couldn't run mcp servers. That's wrong. I wrote mcp-gateway instead, and now those phones reach tools and data the chat surface was never meant to expose. It took an afternoon. No team. No permission. No special access.

That is what bothers me. One keyboard driving many phones, each with its own identity and its own accounts, took me days. If I can do that on my own hardware, so can anyone. Meta should treat it as a real hole and close it, because the next person won't be doing it to learn.

The other half is cost. Some of the muses have their own github accounts, mail logins, chatgpt and deepseek sessions, and the ai-freebuff and ai-opencode tools on the command line. When a job is big they send one large prompt to ai-freebuff or ai-opencode and that CLI does the work. It keeps Meta's usage low. Hand an agent the right accounts and it stops leaning on the expensive one.
