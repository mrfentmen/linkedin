What I didn't have at the time was a way to say that without implying the work was beneath me. I said I wanted to stay close to the technology, which sounded like an excuse.

The version I have now is clearer. I told my next manager that I want to be judged on what I ship and that I know what happens to that number when my week has fifteen meetings in it. He understood and we found a path that kept both.

Pick the work you want to be measured on. Then say that sentence out loud.
