An agent wrote a migration that passed in test and locked a table in production for eleven minutes.

The best postmortem template I've found is four questions and none of them is why.

What changed. What we saw. What we did. What would have caught it. Four lines in a doc, filled in during the incident, not three days after in a meeting where everybody has rehearsed.

I dropped the question why. Why invites a story about a mistake and a person, and the story is always the same, and it never changes the system. What would have caught it is a design question with an answer you can build.

I rewrote our template and the first incident afterwards took forty minutes to document instead of two days. The action items were three, and all three were automated tests, and all three still run.
