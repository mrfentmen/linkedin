I read a paper last week that the headline called a breakthrough and the paper called a correlation in a small sample. Both descriptions were accurate. Only one of them was selling something.

The sample was sixty people over eight weeks. The effect was real within that sample and the confidence interval was wide enough to drive a truck through. The authors were careful about that.
