I fixed a bug yesterday in about four minutes and the reason it was four minutes is that I read the error message before I touched anything.

That's the whole habit. Read the message. Read the line number. Read the second paragraph of the message, which is where the useful sentence sits. Then look at the code.

The older me would open the file and start changing things. That approach feels productive and it costs an hour, because the edits hide the original state and now there are two problems instead of one.

I timed it once out of curiosity. Reading first took two minutes and the fix took ninety seconds. Editing first took about forty minutes on the same class of bug.

There's no cleverness in any of this. The message is the fastest document in the whole system and it's written by the party that knows where the failure happened.
